Open Plant Cost Index

Purpose

The Open Plant Cost Index (OPCI) is a free reference tool designed to provide the key multipliers required for preliminary pre-feasibility cost estimates without the need for expensive paid cost indexes such as the Chemical Engineering Plant Cost Index (CEPCI).

OPCI gives students, small consultancies, researchers and independent estimators transparent, reliable benchmark data.

Output

It delivers two essential, independently reported outputs to adjust historical project data for new designs:

  1. Time (Escalation): Measures how much process plant construction costs have changed between years.
  2. Place (Location Factor): Compares plant construction costs in a specific country relative to the US Gulf Coast.

How the answer was worked out

Escalation uses 12 US Bureau of Labor Statistics price and wage series (public domain), weighted like the published 2002 CEPCI components, with a 2% a year productivity adjustment on labour and engineering. The index level is checked against public CEPCI values. Location factors come from Oxford Institute for Energy Studies group factors (Songhurst 2018, NG 137) and a stated step rule.
Suitable for screening and early estimates (AACE International, the Association for the Advancement of Cost Engineering, Class 5 and Class 4). CEPCI is a trademark of Chemical Engineering magazine; OPCI is not affiliated with or endorsed by it.
